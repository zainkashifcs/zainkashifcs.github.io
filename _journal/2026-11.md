## What the write-up covers

To cover an n by m square with a by a tiles, each side needs that side divided by a, rounded up. The answer is the product of the two counts, with no loop, so it runs in O(1).

The lesson section is three points I want in front of me on every problem:

- `%` is the remainder and `/` is division. Don't mix them up.
- Round with integer arithmetic, not `ceil()`, because floating point can be inaccurate with huge numbers.
- Use long long everywhere. n and m go up to 1e9, so the answer can reach 1e18, and an int stops at about 2.1e9.
