## What the notes say

My notes for 4A are three lines long. The insight is that even + even = even, so the weight w has to be even. The edge case is w = 2, which fails because the only splits are 1 + 1 and 2 + 0. That gives the condition `w % 2 == 0 && w > 2`, and the complexity is O(1).

Short as it is, that note has the shape most of my October notes follow: the idea, the edge case that would break a first attempt, and the complexity.
