# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 6 November 2026**

Welcome to Zain's Blog. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains how information is handled when you visit this website.

## 1. Information Collection

This website does not require visitors to create accounts or provide personal information to browse its content.

Any search, filtering or quiz interactions are designed to run within your browser.

## 2. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies, or third-party visitor-tracking services. Its fonts and scripts are served from this website itself, so browsing it does not load content from other services.

Your theme preference may be saved locally in your browser to remember your preferred appearance. The homepage also remembers which of its three background animations it last showed you, so that your next visit can show a different one. This information stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

## 3. Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with visits as part of providing its hosting services. This is subject to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## 4. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to external websites. I am not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the website's functionality or data practices change.

## 6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can get in touch through [my GitHub profile](https://github.com/zainkashifcs).
