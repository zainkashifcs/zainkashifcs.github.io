# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 November 2026**

Welcome to Zain's Blog. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains how information is handled when you visit this website.

## 1. Information Collection

This website does not require visitors to create accounts or provide personal information to browse its content.

Any search, filtering or quiz interactions are designed to run within your browser.

## 2. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies, or third-party visitor-tracking services. Its fonts and scripts are served from this website itself, so browsing it does not load content from other services.

Your theme preference may be saved locally in your browser to remember your preferred appearance. The homepage also notes, for the current browser tab only, that its opening animation has played, and it remembers in your browser which background you picked (koi, jellyfish or paused). If you use the quizzes, your answers are remembered so you can review your mistakes and see which questions are due for another look: for each question, how often you got it right and wrong, your last result and its date, and when it is next due. You can clear this at any time from the quiz page. If you type notes into the scratchpad on a problem page, they are saved in your browser under that problem (at most 4,000 characters each) so they are still there when you come back; emptying the box deletes them. The contest timer on those pages saves nothing. To let you read pages without a connection, your browser also keeps copies of the pages you have visited and of this site's styles, scripts, fonts and search index, in its own storage on your device; they are replaced when the site is updated, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes them. All of this stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

## 3. Website Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information associated with visits as part of providing its hosting services. This is subject to [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).

## 4. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to external websites. I am not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the website's functionality or data practices change.
